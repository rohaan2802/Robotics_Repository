qz AND qr (Robotics Toolbox)

Both are joint-angle vectors used as reference configurations of a robot.

qz - zero configuration
- All joint angles = 0 -> the robot's home / rest pose.
- e.g. for a 2-joint arm: qz = [0, 0] (links aligned along their zero directions)

qr - a chosen / "ready" configuration
- A specific set of joint angles for a task or target pose.
- e.g. qr = [0.2, 0.3] -> joint 1 at 0.2 rad, joint 2 at 0.3 rad

Where they are used
- Forward kinematics: pass either to fkine to get the end-effector pose in that configuration, e.g. arm.fkine(qz), arm.fkine(qr)
- Trajectory planning / control: move the robot smoothly from qz to qr.

Summary
- qz = [0,0]     -> all joints at zero, home position
- qr = [0.2,0.3] -> target joint angles (rad), used to compute the matching end-effector position
- Both act as reference points for simulation, trajectory planning and control.
