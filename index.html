Overview

Robotics (CS 3015) was taught by Ms. Umarah Qaseem in Fall 2024. The course moves from frames and poses to forward and inverse kinematics, trajectory planning, mobile robots, microcontrollers, ROS, sensors and control, with weekly workshops in Peter Corke's Robotics Toolbox for MATLAB. This repository keeps every slide deck, workshop, assignment and note in the folders I used during the semester.

Unit 1

Pose & transformations

Frames of reference, 2D and 3D rotations, homogeneous transforms, relative pose, Euler angles and roll-pitch-yaw.

Unit 2

Arm kinematics

Joint and robot types, degrees of freedom, Denavit-Hartenberg parameters, forward kinematics and inverse kinematics.

Unit 3

Motion & mobile robots

Cubic and quintic trajectories, trapezoidal profiles, via points, wheel types, mecanum wheels and differential drive.

Unit 4

Hardware, ROS & control

Arduino, ESP32/ESP8266, ROS topics and services, encoders, sonar, IMU, actuators and closed-loop control.
