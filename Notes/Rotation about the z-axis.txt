ROTATION ABOUT THE Z-AXIS

Rotating a point or body around the z-axis of a 3D frame. Sign is set by the right-hand rule.

Positive rotation (+θ)
- Thumb along +z (pointing up), fingers curl in the direction of rotation.
- Seen from the top of the z-axis looking down -> counterclockwise.

Negative rotation (-θ)
- Opposite direction -> clockwise when seen from the top of the z-axis.

Summary
- Positive: counterclockwise about z
- Negative: clockwise about z
