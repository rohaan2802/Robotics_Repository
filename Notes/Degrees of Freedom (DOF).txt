DEGREES OF FREEDOM (DOF)

DOF = number of independent parameters needed to define the position and orientation of a body or mechanism, i.e. the number of independent ways it can move.

Rigid body in 3D -> max 6 DOF
- 3 translational:
  - x-axis: forward / backward
  - y-axis: left / right
  - z-axis: up / down
- 3 rotational:
  - roll  = rotation about x
  - pitch = rotation about y
  - yaw   = rotation about z
So any rigid body in space is fully described by 6 DOF (3 position + 3 orientation).

DOF in robots
- One joint usually gives 1 DOF:
  - revolute joint  -> rotation about one axis
  - prismatic joint -> sliding along one axis
- Total DOF of a manipulator = sum of DOF of all joints.
- To fully control both position and orientation of the end-effector in 3D, the robot needs 6 DOF.

Examples
- 1 DOF: slider moving on a straight track
- 2 DOF: two-link planar arm (2 revolute joints) -> can reach different points in its plane
- 6 DOF: human arm (shoulder, elbow, wrist) -> hand can reach a point in 3D and orient in any direction

Key points
- DOF = number of independent variables that define the configuration.
- 3D space: up to 6 DOF (3 translation + 3 rotation).
- Each joint adds 1 DOF; 6 DOF needed for full position + orientation.
